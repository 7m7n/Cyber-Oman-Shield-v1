# Privacy Policy (Starter)

- This extension Cyber OMAN Shield 2040 v1 runs **locally** in your browser.
- Email content is analyzed **in-page**; no data leaves your device unless you **enable** external API checks.
- If external checks are enabled, **URLs only** (not full email text) may be sent to the selected provider(s) you configured.
- API keys are stored in Chrome/Edge **sync storage**. You may clear them anytime from the Options page or your browser profile settings.
- We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers.
